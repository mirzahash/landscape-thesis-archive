Thesis Submission Brief

What to send me for your thesis page — text, images, and captions, organized the way your page will actually use them.

Your thesis will get its own page in the archive, built to the same structure as the example page already online. You don't need to design anything or size any images — just gather what's below and send it over. If something doesn't apply to your project (no supervisor, no phasing diagram, whatever), leave it out and note that it's missing.

00 / Basics

Project Details

  • Thesis title
    The real title, as it appears on your submitted thesis.
  • One-line summary
    A single sentence describing the project — this runs under your title. Aim for 15–25 words.
  • Your name
  • Site name and location
    e.g. town/region and country. Coordinates are welcome but optional.
  • Supervisor's name
  • Year of submission
01 / Site & Context

Reading the Site

This section introduces the site and the problem your thesis responds to.

  • Site description, 2 short paragraphs
    What the site is, what condition it's in, and why it matters. Around 100–150 words total.
  • Up to 4 key site statistics
    Any hard numbers worth pulling out — area, elevation, rainfall, population, age of existing infrastructure, whatever is central to your site.
    Format: number + unit + one-line label
  • Existing topography / site map
    Image
  • Land-use or context diagram
    Image
  • 2–3 site photographs
    Image

42 ha — site area
02 / Concept & Strategy

The Idea

This section carries your core design argument — the idea everything else follows from.

  • Concept description, 1 paragraph
    Around 80–120 words on your design strategy and reasoning.
  • One pull-quote sentence
    The single sentence that best captures your concept — it runs large on the page.
  • Strategy list, 3–5 items
    Each move in your strategy as a short name plus one-sentence description.
  • Up to 3 diagrams
    Phasing, hydrology/process sections, sketches — whatever explains the thinking.
    Image
Example — strategy list item
Terraced marsh steps — Absorb tidal surge in stages rather than a single wall.

The bulk of your drawings and renders live here. Send everything you have — plans, sections, renders — with a one-line caption for each.

  • Site plan
    Image + scale, if drawn to one
  • Sections

    Image
  • Renders
    Image, highest resolution you have

04 / Reflection

Closing

  • Reflection, 1 short paragraph
    What the thesis argues, and what it leaves unresolved. Around 80–150 words.
  • Final crit poster
    The board you presented at your final review, if you have a clean file of it.
