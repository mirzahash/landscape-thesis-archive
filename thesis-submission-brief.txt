LANDSCAPE THESIS ARCHIVE — VOL. 2026
THESIS SUBMISSION BRIEF

What to send me for your thesis page — text, images, and captions,
organized the way your page will actually use them.

Your thesis will get its own page in the archive, built to the same
structure as the example page already online. You don't need to design
anything or size any images — just gather what's below and send it
over. If something doesn't apply to your project (no supervisor, no
phasing diagram, whatever), leave it out and note that it's missing.


00 / BASICS — PROJECT DETAILS
------------------------------
[ ] Thesis title
    The real title, as it appears on your submitted thesis.

[ ] One-line summary
    A single sentence describing the project — this runs under your
    title. Aim for 15-25 words.

[ ] Your name

[ ] Site name and location
    e.g. town/region and country. Coordinates are welcome but optional.

[ ] Supervisor's name

[ ] Year of submission


01 / SITE & CONTEXT — READING THE SITE
------------------------------
This section introduces the site and the problem your thesis responds to.

[ ] Site description, 2 short paragraphs
    What the site is, what condition it's in, and why it matters.
    Around 100-150 words total.

[ ] Up to 4 key site statistics
    Any hard numbers worth pulling out — area, elevation, rainfall,
    population, age of existing infrastructure, whatever is central
    to your site.
    Format: number + unit + one-line label
    Example: 42 ha — site area

[ ] Existing topography / site map (image)

[ ] Land-use or context diagram (image)

[ ] 2-3 site photographs (image)


02 / CONCEPT & STRATEGY — THE IDEA
------------------------------
This section carries your core design argument — the idea everything
else follows from.

[ ] Concept description, 1 paragraph
    Around 80-120 words on your design strategy and reasoning.

[ ] One pull-quote sentence
    The single sentence that best captures your concept — it runs
    large on the page.

[ ] Strategy list, 3-5 items
    Each move in your strategy as a short name plus one-sentence
    description.
    Example: Terraced marsh steps — Absorb tidal surge in stages
    rather than a single wall.

[ ] Up to 3 diagrams (image)
    Phasing, hydrology/process sections, sketches — whatever
    explains the thinking.


03 / DESIGN PROPOSAL — THE DESIGN
------------------------------
The bulk of your drawings and renders live here. Send everything you
have — plans, sections, renders — with a one-line caption for each.

[ ] Site plan (image + scale, if drawn to one)

[ ] Sections (image)
    As many as are relevant, each labeled (e.g. "Section AA").

[ ] Renders (image, highest resolution you have)

[ ] Optional: existing vs. proposed pair (two images)
    The same view or section, before and after — used for a
    side-by-side comparison on the page.

    Example caption: Fig. 08 — Section AA, marsh terrace — scale 1:200


04 / REFLECTION — CLOSING
------------------------------
[ ] Reflection, 1 short paragraph
    What the thesis argues, and what it leaves unresolved.
    Around 80-150 words.

[ ] Final crit poster (image, portrait orientation)
    The board you presented at your final review, if you have a
    clean file of it.


FILE FORMATS
------------------------------
Images       JPG or PNG. Longest edge at least 2000px. Send the
             highest resolution file you have — it can always be
             scaled down.

Text         .docx, .txt, or in the body of your email. Plain text
             is fine, no need to format it.

File names   01_site-plan.jpg — number your images in the order
             you'd like them to appear, with a short description.


SENDING IT OVER
------------------------------
Once everything's gathered, send it as a single folder or zip so
nothing gets separated from its caption.
